## How to be listed

Everyone who helps can be listed — not only programmers:

- **teachers** — advice on tasks, levels and the curriculum;
- **translators** — new languages and corrections by native speakers;
- **families** — trying the sheets with children and telling us what was hard (an issue is enough);
- **developers and designers** — pull requests.

Add yourself in your pull request: one line in this table and one entry in `site/contributors.json`
(roles: `idea`, `teacher`, `translation`, `code`, `testing`, `design`). If you helped without a pull request,
write in your issue that you would like to be listed, and the maintainer will add you.

Only the name or nickname **you choose**, with your consent. Never e-mail addresses, and never children's names.
