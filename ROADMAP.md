# Roadmap: bringing everything up to the standard

What does not yet follow [STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md), in order of impact. Rule numbers refer to the standard.
Want to help? Pick an item and open an issue first.

## 1. Year 1 review tasks (used in review sheets, Year 2 and topic sheets) — biggest gap

- [ ] **I1, D1** — 17 of the 18 review tasks have no action icon and no solved first item. Add both.
- [ ] **D2** — order items from easy to harder after the example (also in the Reception and Year 1 autumn tasks).
- [ ] **T2** — child-facing text is 17–22 px (task titles 21 px, sentences 19 px, table text 19 px, word bank 17 px); needs ≥ 23 px.
- [ ] **L3** — review pages hold 3–6 tasks (page 1: 6); Year 1 allows 3. The 18-task review sheet must become shorter
      (for example two sheets of 9 tasks, or presets of up to 12 topics) — **decision needed**.
- [ ] **W1** — several instructions are longer than 8 words per sentence (e.g. task 17: "Look at the picture and complete
      the sentences with the positional words."); shorten in all languages.
